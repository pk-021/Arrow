About Arrow data

Arrow stores its settings and session information in Chrome storage.

Eligible preferences sync through Chrome when Sync is available. Arrow does not send data to Arrow-operated servers and does not use a separate Arrow account.

Uninstalling Arrow removes its local data. Chrome-managed synced preferences may be restored if Chrome retains them and Arrow is reinstalled.
